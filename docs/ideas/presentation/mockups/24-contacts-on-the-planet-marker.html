Contacts on the planet marker CANDIDATE

The fold marker goes for planets. The second ring behind the planet's marker (the moon ring) takes the affiliation of the most pressing contact folded in, and the label's second line gives the count. In Sol here: Jupiter and Ceres hold moons only; Earth holds own ships; Saturn a hostile ship; Uranus an unknown contact. Real pixel size.

A · Ring in the contact's colour

Colour only. The ring keeps its circle shape and takes the colour, with the symbol fill mix (SYM-03). Quietest, and readable at a glance; shape is left to the inset.

B · Ring in the contact's frame shape and colour

The NATO frame at the planet's own size. Diamond for hostile, quatrefoil for unknown, square for neutral and allied, circle for own: the same shapes as a symbol, so it reads without colour.

C · Neutral ring, a glyph on the label

The ring says only "something is folded in". Tiny affiliation glyphs sit before the count on the label's second line, one per affiliation present.
