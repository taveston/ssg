Interface style: panels, windows and reports

CANDIDATE One console register throughout. Reports are written by people and read like signals. Corvane, 14 Mar 2341.

1 · Figures carry their certainty

The same notation in every panel, report and table, so the player can always tell measured from guessed.

141 kExact: measured or own data. ~4Estimate: one best guess. 6–8Range: bounds known, value not. ?Unknown: never observed. 4 · A report: written by someone

Signal header, then plain prose from the author's point of view, then the data it rests on. The author's traits colour the prose; the figures under it are the state's own record.

PRECIMMEDIATE DTG14 MAR 2341 0612 FROMCDR TF-1 (I. MARR) TOADMIRALTY · INFO GOV HALDEN IV SUBJCONTACT REPORT · K-3

Weak thermal track K-3 holding a slow orbit off Halden IV since 09 Mar. Emissions fit a medium hauler running cold. I do not believe a hauler would bother.

5 · A request: options with known and unknown costs

Options are explicit. Costs the state can price are figures; consequences it can't are stated as the author's claim, not as numbers.

8 · One register or two?

The admin view could switch to a "paper" look. Recommended against: the console register is cheaper, reads better at length on a dark screen, and keeps the map one click away without a jolt. Paper flavour comes from structure (signal headers, file stamps), not colour.
