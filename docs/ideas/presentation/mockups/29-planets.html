Procedural planet surfaces: seven kinds, how much is known, and light CANDIDATE

Planet surfaces drawn by a shader from the body's own figures, with no art assets: kind, gravity, equilibrium temperature, atmosphere class and water, plus a seed. The seed would come from the sector seed and the body's id, so a body looks the same each time it is inspected. The same shader carries "fidelity follows knowledge": fewer noise octaves and no small craters give a body seen from far away, and blur and grain on top give the telescope frame. Surface colours, atmospheres and the figures printed on the sheet are placeholders for tuning. Rings, clouds on rocky worlds and sub-Neptunes are left out; a sub-Neptune would use the ice-giant look with a paler envelope.
