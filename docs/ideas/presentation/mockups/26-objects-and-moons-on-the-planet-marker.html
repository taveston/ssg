Objects and moons on the planet marker CANDIDATE

The moon ring is neutral and says only "there are moons" (the dim "+N" counts moons). Objects are the things you select, so they get their own marker at rest, down and to the right of the planet, mirroring the ring. Two resting forms: A, a 12 px frame in the affiliation shape and colour with the count inside; B, a 6 px pip with no count. Either grows into the full 22 px multi-marker below the planet when the pointer comes near or the planet is selected. Real pixel size; the strips enlarge 5 times.

A · At rest: small frame with a count

Reads as an object even at overview. Shape and colour say friend or foe; the number says how many. Earth: 1 moon, 2 own ships. Saturn: 2 moons, 2 hostile ships.

B · At rest: pip, no count

Quieter. Shape and colour only; the count waits for the pointer. Two pips of one planet would not be told apart from one.

A · Pointer near Saturn

The frame grows into the marker below the planet. The ring stays where it was and stays neutral. The dashed circle (not drawn in the game) is the 24 px reach.

B · Pointer near Saturn

The pip grows into the same marker. A bigger jump in size, and the count appears with it.
