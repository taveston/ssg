Resting pips for mixed objects CANDIDATE

The front pip takes the most pressing affiliation among the objects folded into the planet (hostile, unknown, neutral or allied, own). A back pip, 2 px right and up, is own when any object is own and otherwise a copy of the front; it is the "more than one" signal. A single object has no back pip. The moon ring is always neutral.
