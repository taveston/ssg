Several objects, one symbol idiom CANDIDATE

One idiom for anything that stands for several objects: a normal frame, the count where the icon would be, a second frame behind to say "several". It replaces the fold pill (GRP-05, GRP-06) and the loose-group stack (SYM-09). It also covers symbols that would overlap on screen. Picking a member is done in a tray at the marker, not only in the inset's outline. Names are placeholders.

Today's pill and the proposed marker

Beside a planet, the marker replaces the pill. The front frame is the most pressing affiliation, as the pill's outline colour is now (GRP-06), so the shape reads without colour. Own ships show as the frame behind. A neutral ship among hostile ones is not shown, as today.

One idiom for several objects

In context: Halden IV's High band, paused, 14 ships

Today. Symbols within a frame's width of each other pile up, and their labels are hidden for want of room (LBL-01, LBL-03). The ring here is about 220 px across. Smaller, it is worse.

Proposed. Symbols that would overlap collapse into one marker. The marker with the selection ring is open: its tray lists the four members, and the row you step to is selected, so the inset follows it. Single ships keep their labels.
