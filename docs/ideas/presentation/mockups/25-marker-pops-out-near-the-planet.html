Marker pops out near the planet CANDIDATE

The same Sol overview in three states. At rest, a planet with things folded into it shows only its ring and a dim "+N"; the ring takes the colour of the most pressing contact. When the pointer comes within about 24 px of the planet, the ring grows into the full multi-marker below it. A click on the marker opens its tray. Saturn here holds two moons and a hostile ship. Real pixel size; the strip enlarges the growth 4 times.

1 · At rest

Rings only. Nothing next to the planets to compete with them; labels and the count line never change.

2 · Pointer near Saturn

The ring grows into the marker, below the planet. The dashed circle (not drawn in the game) is the 24 px reach. Hostile front frame, the count of three. It also shows while the planet is selected, so a keyboard, gamepad or touch player gets it without a pointer.

3 · Click on the marker

The tray opens to its right, clear of the planet and its label, as it does for any multi-marker (MUL-08).
